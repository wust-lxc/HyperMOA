# Third-party sources and attribution

STAIR: Yu and Xie, Spatial transcriptomic alignment, integration, and 3D
reconstruction by STAIR, Genome Biology 26, 427 (2025).
https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-025-03895-x
https://github.com/yuyuanyuana/STAIR

The spot/section attention and the initialization/boundary alignment workflow
are attributed to STAIR in the manuscript and release documentation. The
upstream repository identifies the MIT license. Its license text is retained
under third_party/ when downloaded. The release does not relicense upstream
components or assign a new license to the author's contributions.

Comparison-method packages are installed from their own upstream repositories;
their full repositories are not redistributed here. BASELINE_SOURCES.json
records the local repository commits/remotes and whether changes were present.
Public datasets retain their source attribution in DATASETS.csv.

Before public publication the author supplies the authors/affiliations and
confirms the license for the HyperMOA additions and derived data. The current
repository did not contain a project LICENSE file. Those publication metadata
have not been invented in this local preparation.
